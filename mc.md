2. Log in
3. Click **Installations**
4. Click **New installation**
5. Name it anything
6. Click *"Latest release"*
7. Type `release 1.20.1`
8. Deselect **Launch After Install**
9. Click **Install**
10. Click the folder icon
11. Delete everything
12. Download the installation from [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/11c_4Usu_2sOXC5gNpR2EIzSevjCO9IYg?usp=sharing)
13. Drag and drop everything
14. Click **Play**
15. Click **Multiplayer**
16. Click **Add Server**
17. Fill <details>IP: 1mpw.aternos.me<summary>details</summary></details> in
